# Affon e2e coverage inventory

This inventory tracks public behavior, not test counts or PyTorch comparisons.
A linked test establishes only the behavior stated in its row. `Missing` means
there is no assertion for that aspect yet; it does not mean the implementation
is broken. Package and app workflows remain with their owners.

## Review rule

A new public workflow needs a success assertion and an explicit decision for
invalid input, recovery, and repeated use. Keep numerical peer comparisons in
`test/parity/`. Do not count a shape-only or finite-value assertion as recovery
or persistence coverage.
