Leo wanted to help Benny. He said, "Don't worry, Benny. I will help you find your nest and eggs." So, Leo and Benny looked all around the forest. They climbed trees and searched high and low. Finally, they found Benny's nest with the eggs safe inside.
Benny was so happy and said, "Thank you, Leo! You are a good friend." Leo was happy too because he could help his friend. From that day on, Benny was not angry anymore and they both became best friends.
The moral of the story is to always help your friends when they are in need, and good things will happen.
